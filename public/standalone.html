Atelier Philosophy

Our Mission: Banishing Hollow Plastics

Nocturne was founded with a singular conviction: the tools you touch for thousands of hours each year should possess the permanence, acoustic dignity, and precision of a Swiss mechanical movement. We mill solid 6063 aerospace aluminum, suspend PCB assemblies with isolated silicone gasket dampers, and hand-balance stainless stabilizer wires.

Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Nocturne mechanical keyboards hot-swappable?

Yes. Every board features Kailh hot-swap sockets rated for over 100 switch swaps with zero soldering required.

How does 8000Hz polling rate work?

High-speed 32-bit ARM microcontrollers transmit reports at 0.125ms intervals directly via USB-C or 4K wireless dongles.
